# Target selection and evidence

Checked 13 November 2026. New family: industrial-hygiene. The existing Cority target is occupational-health and its health-monitoring repo covers clinical monitoring. This build covers group-level sampling evidence, review history and control follow-up. It does not carry clinical decisions, measurement equipment or regulatory certification.

US$40,112 is a documented increase in annual maintenance for Industrial Hygiene, Ergonomics and myCority together in Orange County Sanitation District agenda 2020-1311, approved in November 2020. It is historical US purchasing evidence of a five-figure bill, not an IH-only price, current quote or actual 10 to 50 person NZ/AU invoice. The annual-bill score is deliberately conservative. Cority's current pricing page describes scoped subscriptions and implementation without a public IH dollar amount. The public comparison therefore uses no dollar figure.

Searches of the available customer files and CRM-related paths found no supporting customer record; no live customer system was read. Cority describes Australian occupational hygiene features on its own site, which establishes market relevance, not that our buyers use it. Search results contain rival lists and software discussion; they do not establish query volume. VelocityEHS's product page establishes the category, but a priced annual bill and exact import contract were not verified. SlateSafety's pricing page was inaccessible and a useful monitoring replacement would require equipment connections, so it failed the buildability screen.
